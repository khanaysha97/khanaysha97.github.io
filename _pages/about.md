I hold a Ph.D. in Applied Mathematics, with specialization in Queueing Theory, Stochastic Modelling, and Operations Research. I'm working as a Research Associate at the Indian Institute of Technology Delhi (IIT Delhi), where my research focused on hyperspectral imaging, computational data analysis, and probabilistic hyperspectral band selection using k-Determinantal Point Processes (k-DPPs). Please refer to my [curriculum vitae](/files/cv.pdf) for more details about my academic and research journey.
